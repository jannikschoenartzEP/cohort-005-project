# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### Refunds

**Reviewer**:
Someone who decides Refund Requests: an Admin, for any course, or an Instructor, for their own courses only. Only Admins carry out the Refund that follows.
_Avoid_: Team (a Team is a group of learners sharing a team licence), Support

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. A Reviewer approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Reviewer has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Reviewer has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and the learner loses access to the course at that moment. The decision is final.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Reviewer has turned down, with a short reason the learner can see. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund. Refunding a team licence also makes its unclaimed seats unusable.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund where an Admin hasn't yet confirmed the money as sent.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed, and the learner stays without access. A Failed Refund can be retried, which makes it Pending again.

### Coming back after a Refund

**Starting Over**:
When a learner buys a course again after a Refund, they can start over, which hides their earlier progress from them. Their earlier watching still counts when they next raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Reset, Wipe

**Continuing**:
When a learner buys a course again after a Refund, they can continue, picking up their earlier progress where they left it.
_Avoid_: Resume (for this choice)
